Nahem Assistant: an Arabic-first ordering chatbot for a Madinah restaurant

I built this assistant for Nahem, a 24-hour burger restaurant with two branches in Madinah. It answers customers in Arabic or English, recommends meals, plans group orders, and turns a conversation into a WhatsApp order. The chatbot on this page is the real one.

What it does

🧭 Guided ordering"Help me order" asks for group size and taste, then builds the basket.
🛒 Chat-to-WhatsApp ordersAdd items with + or by typing "add 2 zinger", pick a branch, send in one tap.
👥 Group & budget planner"We are 9" or "I only have 50 riyals" gives priced options per person.
🍔 Smart recommendationsBy time of day in Madinah, taste (spicy, chicken, kids…) and best sellers.
🔥 Calories & honestyExact calories from the menu; says "I don't know" instead of inventing facts.
✍️ Typo & dialect tolerant"reccomeed", "الفروووع", Gulf dialect and formal Arabic all understood.
🎙️ Voice, photos, dark modeSpeak Arabic or English, food photos on every card, works on phones.
📈 Learns from customersUnanswered questions, 👍/👎 and orders are logged to a Google Sheet.

How it's built

  • One drop-in script (vanilla JavaScript, Shadow DOM) added to the restaurant's static site with a single <script> tag.
  • Own NLU engine: Arabic normalisation, typo tolerance (Levenshtein + consonant skeletons), intent priorities, follow-up context, entity parsing for quantities, budgets and group sizes.
  • Live data: reads prices and branches from the client's admin panel (Google Apps Script CMS), so menu edits reach the bot instantly.
  • Optional AI layer: a Google Apps Script backend calls Gemini with the live menu as context; the built-in engine is the fallback.
  • Tested with 400+ real-style customer questions, regression-diffed on every change.
